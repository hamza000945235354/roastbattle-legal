Delete your account

This page works whether or not the app is installed.

You can delete your Roast Battle account and the data attached to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app

Fastest, and takes effect immediately.

  • Open Roast Battle
  • Go to Profile
  • Tap Settings
  • Tap Delete my account
  • Confirm

Option 2 — By email

Use this if you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in.

Send us this

To: delete@hamzalabs.com

Subject: Delete my Roast Battle account

Include:

  • Your username, for example @ayan_92
  • The email address or phone number on the account

Send it from the email address registered to the account. If you signed up with a phone number, we will send a verification code to that number before deleting anything.

We confirm within 72 hours and complete deletion within 30 days.

Before you delete

  • Unspent coins are forfeited. They are not refunded, in coins or in money.
  • This cannot be undone. Deleted accounts cannot be restored.
  • Your username is released and someone else may take it.
  • Clips you already shared to TikTok, Instagram or elsewhere stay there. You must remove those yourself on each platform.

Just want a break?

You do not have to delete everything. You can also:

  • Turn off notifications in Settings
  • Delete individual roasts and keep your account
  • Block anyone you do not want to be matched with

Not sure? Email support@hamzalabs.com and we will help you work out what you actually need.
