Child Safety Standards

Published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)
Last updated: 27 August 2026

Roast Battle is operated by Muhammad Hamza, Lahore, Pakistan. This page sets out our standards, systems and obligations for preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation on our service.

Our position

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and for any attempt to sexualise, groom, exploit or endanger a minor.

There is no warning, no strike system and no appeal for this category. Accounts are removed permanently on first detection, content is preserved for law enforcement, and the incident is reported to the relevant authorities.

1. Roast Battle is an adults-only service

Roast Battle is rated for adults and is restricted to users aged 18 and over. We do not offer a version of this app for children, and it is not designed for or directed at minors.

  • Every user must confirm they are 18 or older before they can post anything. This confirmation is unskippable and is recorded with a timestamp.
  • Our Google Play target audience is set to 18 and over.
  • The app does not appear in Google Play's Family programme and is not eligible for it.
  • If we learn or reasonably suspect that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close the account and delete the associated data.

2. What is prohibited

The following are prohibited absolutely, in any format, including audio, video, text, username, avatar or shared link:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Sexualisation of a minor, including through description, implication or "joke"
  • Grooming, or any attempt to build a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes
  • Soliciting, offering, trading, or linking to CSAM
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share a minor's images
  • Attempting to move a minor to another platform or to private contact
  • Content that presents a minor in a manner intended to elicit sexual interest

These prohibitions apply regardless of the poster's intent, and regardless of whether the content is framed as comedy. Comedy is not a defence.

3. How we prevent and detect it

3.1 Screening before publication

Every voice and video recording is screened before it becomes visible to any other user. Nothing is published first and reviewed later.

Our pipeline runs in four layers:

  • Automated transcription of all audio into text
  • Keyword and pattern matching against prohibited terms, in English, Roman Urdu and Urdu script
  • Automated classification of ambiguous content, including detection of sexual content involving minors
  • Human review of anything flagged by the layers above

Content that fails screening is never published and is retained for investigation.

3.2 Design choices that reduce risk

  • No private messaging. The app has no direct message feature, which removes the most common grooming channel.
  • No follower relationships and no way to accumulate a personal fan base.
  • No location sharing. We do not collect or share precise location.
  • No exchange of contact details. Attempts to share phone numbers, handles or off-platform links are treated as violations.
  • Adults-only gate before any content can be posted.

4. Reporting inside the app

Every piece of user-generated content and every user profile carries an in-app report option, available to all users without leaving the app.

  • Reports include a dedicated child safety category
  • Reports in that category are escalated immediately, ahead of the normal queue
  • Users can also block any other user, as a function separate from reporting
  • Reporting requires no account verification and no cost

Anyone may also report directly to hamzasiddique951@gmail.com with the subject line Child safety.

6. Reporting to authorities

We comply with all applicable child safety laws and report confirmed CSAE to the relevant regional and national authorities. Depending on where the content and the user are located, this includes:

  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline
  • The Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) Cyber Crime Wing, Pakistan
  • Any other competent national authority with jurisdiction over the account or the content

We cooperate fully with lawful requests from law enforcement, and we preserve relevant data on request.
