Common problems

I bought coins and did not get them

Coins normally arrive within a few seconds. If they have not:

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Check your internet connection
  • Go to Profile → Settings → Restore purchases

Still missing? Email us with your Google Play order ID, which is in your Play Store purchase receipt. We will credit the coins manually.

I want a refund

All purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds go through Google, not us. Request one at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory within 48 hours of purchase.

Note that if a refund is granted, the corresponding coins are removed from your balance.

My roast was blocked

Every recording is screened before it goes public. Roasts are blocked when they contain slurs, threats, sexual content, or attacks on someone's family, appearance, religion or ethnicity.

If you think the decision was wrong, email hamzasiddique951@gmail.com with the approximate time you posted it. We review appeals within 7 days.

My account was suspended or banned

Email hamzasiddique951@gmail.com from the address on the account. Explain what happened. We will review and reply within 7 days.

Bans for threats, doxxing, or sexual content involving minors are permanent and not reversed.

Someone is harassing me

Use the report button on their roast or profile, and the block button to stop being matched with them. We review reports within 24 hours.

If you feel you are in danger, contact your local authorities first, then email hamzasiddique951@gmail.com.

My recording will not upload

  • Check that Roast Battle has microphone permission in your device settings
  • For video, also check camera permission
  • Video needs a stable connection. Try Wi-Fi.
  • Recordings are capped at 20 seconds

My battle has not finished

Battles need a minimum number of votes before a round can be called. If not enough people voted before the window closed, the AI judge decides it instead. You can speed this up by sharing your battle link so more people judge it.

I did not get my judging coins

Coins for judging are awarded only when you actually listen to both roasts, and there is a daily limit. Skipping a battle earns nothing. This keeps voting honest.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all your content from this page, or in the app under Profile → Settings.

Unspent coins are forfeited on deletion and are not refunded.

  • Your device model and Android version
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one
