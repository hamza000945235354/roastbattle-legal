Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026  ·  Effective: 27 August 2026

This policy explains what Roast Battle collects, why, who else sees it, and how you get rid of it. Roast Battle ("we", "the app") is operated by HamzaLabs, Pakistan.

Because this app records your voice and, optionally, your video, please read section 2 carefully.

1. Who this applies to

Roast Battle is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we close it and delete the data.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account information

DataWhy
Email address or phone numberTo create and secure your account

If you sign in with Google, Facebook or Snapchat, we receive your email address and basic profile from that provider. We do not receive your password, and we do not post anything to those accounts.

2.2 Voice and video recordings

Read this part

When you record a roast, the audio (and video, if you chose video mode) is uploaded to our servers. It is automatically transcribed into text and screened for harmful content before anyone else can see or hear it.

Transcription and screening are performed by third-party AI providers. Your recording leaves our servers to make this possible. See section 4.

We keep the recording, the transcript, and the moderation result. The transcript is retained even if the recording is deleted, because we need it to handle reports, appeals and any legal request.

The app requests microphone access only while you are recording, and camera access only in video mode. You can refuse either, and you can revoke both in your device settings at any time. Refusing the camera does not prevent you from using voice battles.

2.3 Activity in the app

  • Battles you take part in, and the result
  • Votes you cast when judging other people's battles
  • Coin transactions: what you earned, what you spent, and when
  • Gifts sent and received
  • Referral code usage
  • Reports you file and reports filed against you

2.4 Purchases

Coin purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card number, CVV, or billing address. We receive a purchase token, the product bought, and the amount, which we use to credit your coins and to handle refunds.

2.5 Device and technical data

  • Device model, operating system version, app version
  • A device identifier, used to detect referral abuse and duplicate accounts
  • IP address, used for security, fraud prevention and approximate country
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Push notification token, if you enable notifications

We do not collect precise GPS location, your contacts, your photo library, your calendar, or your SMS messages.

5. What other users can see

Public: your username, avatar, rank, win and loss record, and any roast of yours that passes moderation, including its audio, video and transcript. Battles you publish can be viewed and voted on by anyone using the app.

Not public: your email, phone number, coin balance, purchase history, and any report you file.

Shared clips. When you share a winning clip to TikTok, Instagram, or anywhere else, it leaves Roast Battle and we no longer control it. Once you post it publicly, we cannot take it back from those platforms.

7. Your rights

  • Access a copy of your data
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your account and content
  • Withdraw consent for microphone, camera or notifications, in device settings
  • Object to a moderation decision by appealing it in the app

To exercise any of these, email support@hamzalabs.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in two ways:

  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account
  • On the web: the account deletion page, which works even after you uninstall

Deletion removes your profile, recordings, transcripts, votes and coin balance within 30 days. Unspent coins are forfeited and are not refunded. Purchase records and ban records are retained as described in section 6.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access to production data is limited to authorised personnel. Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

10. Children

Roast Battle is rated for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the app, contact support@hamzalabs.com and we will investigate and remove the account.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will notify you in the app before it takes effect. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
